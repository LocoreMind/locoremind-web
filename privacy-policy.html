Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

LocoreMind ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our local AI models and services.

2. Local-First Philosophy

Our models are designed to run entirely on your local hardware. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your machine when using our models locally
  • We do not collect, store, or process your prompts or model outputs
  • You maintain complete control over your data and privacy
  • No API calls are made to our servers during model inference

3. Website Analytics

Our website (locoremind.com) may collect basic analytics information including:

  • Page views and navigation patterns
  • Browser type and device information
  • Referring websites
  • General geographic location (country/region level)

This information is used solely to improve our website and understand how users interact with our content.

4. GitHub and Third-Party Services

Our code repositories are hosted on GitHub and models on HuggingFace. When you interact with these platforms:

  • GitHub's privacy policy applies to repository interactions
  • HuggingFace's privacy policy applies to model downloads
  • We do not control or have access to data collected by these platforms

5. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for:

  • Remembering your dark mode preference
  • Basic analytics (if enabled)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.

6. Data Security

Since our models run locally on your hardware, you are responsible for:

  • Securing your local environment
  • Protecting any sensitive data you process with our models
  • Implementing appropriate access controls
  • Backing up your data as needed

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us.

8. International Users

Our models can be used anywhere in the world. Since they run locally, data processing occurs entirely within your jurisdiction, subject to your local laws and regulations.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository at github.com/LocoreMind.

Privacy-First Commitment

At LocoreMind, privacy isn't just a policy—it's our architecture. By running models locally on your hardware, we've eliminated the fundamental privacy risks associated with cloud-based AI services. Your code, your data, your machine. Always.
